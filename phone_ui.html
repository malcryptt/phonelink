📱 PhoneLink Manual

Welcome to PhoneLink! This is a comprehensive headless IoT automation toolkit designed to completely manage your Android device remotely without relying on graphical apps.

🔗 Connections

Wi-Fi Pair: Click the Wi-Fi Pair button to enable wireless ADB. Once you unplug your USB, simply ensure the laptop IP is set at the top, and all controls will run wirelessly.

> phonelink wifi

Fix ADB: Click the little wrench if things get stuck. It kills the background ADB servers and restarts them automatically.

> phonelink fix

☎️ Calls & audio

Hear on PC: Next-gen feature! When your phone rings, press Answer, then press Hear on PC. This magically tunnels your phone’s speaker output and microphone directly through your laptop. You can leave the phone in the other room and just talk through your laptop.

> phonelink call audio

Call Guard: By typing phonelink guard in your terminal, your laptop will quietly watch your phone. If anyone calls, your laptop pops up a notification and auto-screenshots the caller ID to your desktop.

> phonelink guard

💬 SMS & Typing

Universal Type: The text injection field acts as a magic keyboard. Select a text input on your phone screen, type here, and press inject to instantly paste long passwords or URLs.

> phonelink type "Hello World"

URL Opener: Paste any link and hit open. PhoneLink forces Android to open it in Chrome/YouTube seamlessly.

> adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW -d https://...

Inbox Explorer: Dumps your recent text messages straight into the laptop terminal. Perfect if you’re waiting for a 2FA code and don't want to dig out the phone.

> phonelink inbox

💡 Flashlight & Media

Did you drop a coin under the couch? Use the Flash ON / Flash OFF buttons to turn your phone into a remote control lantern. The Volume controls easily adjust playback entirely from your web UI.

> phonelink shell cmd statusbar expand-settings

🎛️ Power Management

Full bidirectional control. If your phone battery is dying, you can remotely shut it off from here. If you are sitting on the couch and want to shut down your laptop, you can literally tap Shutdown Laptop right from this HTML page on your phone browser!

> phonelink power reboot / phonelink power shutdown

💻 Desktop Dashboard Additions

Terminal: Captures all CLI outputs from the laptop execution automatically.

App Drawer: Lists and manages installed apps dynamically.

> phonelink app com.android.chrome

Gallery: Pulls the newest camera photos from internal storage to view remotely.

> phonelink pull /sdcard/DCIM/Camera

Port Forwarding: Sets up TCP port bridges over adb for mesh networking.

> phonelink forward 8080 8000
